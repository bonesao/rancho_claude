# Document overview

This document tracks the current state of the vegetable beds plus a
running activity log. Updated whenever something is planted, harvested,
fails, or otherwise changes status.

Organized by **named growing area/sector** (as of 2026-09-11) rather than
by crop, since that's how the areas are tracked on the ground. A visual
layout/map is planned eventually (not yet built) — these names are the
source of truth until then.

For planting timing and per-crop notes, see
`vegetable_planting_calendar.md` (or `calendario_huerta.md` in Spanish).

# Planned — bolsón de obra bed-build roadmap (started 2026-08-03)

Eleven crops originally scoped for the new bolsón bags: broccoli (new
batch), kale, sunchoke, eggplant, pepper, tomato, pumpkin (×2 uses),
luffa, corn, sunflower, zucchini. General planting windows and per-crop
tips live in `vegetable_planting_calendar.md` /
`calendario_huerta.md`.

**Lesson learned (2026-09-11)**: the plan called for starting several of
these (broccoli, eggplant, pepper, tomato) in the greenhouse before
transplanting. That step was skipped due to time constraints — everything
went straight into bags 1/2 as direct sow instead. Broccoli, chard, and
beet in bag 2 are doing fine "post-baby," suggesting direct sow can work
for at least some of these in this climate/season — worth weighing against
the greenhouse-start recommendation next time rather than treating it as
mandatory.

# Notes / lessons to revisit

- **2026 tomato + pepper underperformance (earlier, undated planting)**:
  planted too late in summer/autumn to ripen before cooling temps slowed
  growth, and didn't survive winter. For future solanaceae: start seeds
  in greenhouse mid-July, transplant the first cohort early-to-mid
  October (per the planting calendar). The 2026-09-11 tomato/pepper
  sowing in the old raised bed is a fresh, unrelated planting.
- **Direct-sow vs. greenhouse-start for the bolsón batch**: skipping the
  greenhouse-start step for broccoli/eggplant/pepper/tomato in bags 1/2
  didn't visibly hurt broccoli/chard/beet (doing fine post-baby in bag
  2) — worth tracking eggplant/pepper/tomato outcomes here too before
  concluding direct sow is fine across the board.
- **Kale (high raised bed)**: sown at an unlogged date, not seen growing.
  Check in person whether it's dead or just slow before writing it off.
